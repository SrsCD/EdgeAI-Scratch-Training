# Stage 2E — unified six-class candidate dataset audit

**23 November 2026.** Scope: four newly imported Roboflow ZIPs plus the four Stage 2D archives, all within `EdgeAI-Scratch-Training`. The Stage 2D ledger was reused; its 2,435 images were not re-decoded. No original ZIP was modified, no new dataset was downloaded, no pretrained weight was loaded, and no training ran. The fixed target IDs are **0 person, 1 helmet, 2 safety_vest, 3 backpack, 4 knife, 5 pistol**.

## Outcome

There are **7,006 newly imported candidate images**, and **9,441 candidate images** across the eight audited ZIPs. **Accepted = 0**: no image has passed six-class annotation review, rights/provenance review, and duplicate/source-group resolution. The versioned [unified staging manifest](../data/interim/stage2e_unified_v0/unified_candidate_manifest_v0.csv) references all 9,441 source images without claiming that they are training-ready. No images or labels were promoted to `data/processed`, and no final split was released.

## Duplicates, source groups and imported split risk

SHA-256 found **zero exact image duplicates** within the 7,006 new images and **zero exact matches** to the 2,435 Stage 2D images. With a 64-bit pHash Hamming threshold ≤4, the new set has **689 candidate near-duplicate pairs** (40 Site Safety, 80 construction PPE, 231 backpack, 338 pistols); **172 cross source-provided split boundaries**. No new-to-Stage-2D pHash pair met this threshold. The [new pair list](../reports/stage2e_unified/near_duplicate_candidates.csv) and [cross-Stage-2D comparison](../reports/stage2e_unified/cross_stage2d_overlap_candidates.csv) record the check. A pHash match is a review candidate, not confirmed duplication, and zero matches do not exclude crops, rotations, scene overlaps or resizes.

Filename/video grouping finds **827 multi-image new-source group hints**, **441 crossing source splits**. Of these, 810 groups (433 cross-split) are in construction PPE, where repeated filename stems often show the same worker/room from different angles; 17 groups (8 cross-split) are in Site Safety. [Group hints](../reports/stage2e_unified/related_source_groups.csv) need human adjudication. The construction PPE export has 2,355 images but only **1,211 distinct pre-Roboflow filename stems**, which are *not* a verified count of unique source photos. Backpack has 963 images but 592 distinct stems; [same-stem comparison](../reports/stage2e_unified/backpack_roboflow_same_stem_comparison.jpg) shows some stems reused for **different products**, so those were **not** automatically grouped. The [PPE same-stem comparison](../reports/stage2e_unified/construction_ppe_roboflow_same_stem_comparison.jpg) shows related scene views. Source READMEs claim no applied augmentation; repeated stems and near pairs still require original-source and transformation review.

Preserve original source split as metadata only. Before final splitting, visually resolve pair candidates, identify source video/session/original image IDs and any generated variants, join related image families, then assign complete groups. Prior Stage 2D also found 1,444 near-pair candidates and source-split leakage risks. The eight-ZIP audit did **not** compare against the separate 187 Stage 2B/2C quarantined pilot images; that overlap check remains required if those images enter the unified pool.

## Rights and provenance blockers

ZIP YAMLs/READMEs and the live project pages state **CC BY 4.0** for the three new construction/backpack exports and **Public Domain** for Pistols. These are source-level claims. No per-image creator, original URL/license history, consent or redistribution evidence was provided in the ZIPs. Product photos, stock watermarks, composites and screen captures in the visual sample require individual provenance checks. In particular, the Pistols README says it rehosts University of Grenada material and includes cartoons and staged images; the claimed Public Domain badge does not by itself establish the rights of each underlying image. The original university source page could not be verified during this audit. Preserve required attribution and change notices for any image eventually accepted. Rights verified = **0** in the manifest.

## Staging, remaining gap and final split gate

The [staging area](../data/interim/stage2e_unified_v0/README.md) contains a versioned manifest only, with `accepted=No`, `rights_verified=No`, `six_class_complete=No`, `duplicate_group_resolved=No`, and no target label or split. It records the source archive/member path, original source IDs in the mapping table, hashes, dimensions, original split, source annotations, candidate class and group hint. The [classification CSV](../reports/stage2e_unified/candidate_acceptance_and_omission_status.csv) separates 9,132 quarantined candidates from 309 game-source rejects; neither is accepted. The 187 Stage 2B/2C pilot candidates are separate and remain quarantined.

There are **9,441 ZIP candidates**, so even the unrealistic all-accepted upper bound is **559 short** of 10,000 unique images. Excluding the 1,432 older motorcycle/game/headwear images with no bulk-compatible labels leaves at most **8,009** knife-plus-new candidates, **1,991 short**, before other rejections or duplicate grouping. The measured gap from the current **zero accepted** pool is **10,000**. All six independent-positive class targets (≥1,500 where achievable) remain unverified; source counts above do not close them. Seek additional rights-traceable, diverse images only after the review yield is measured. No new source was downloaded in this stage.

The [split feasibility report](../reports/stage2e_unified/split_feasibility.md) shows zero accepted images in train/test/validation, not invented final split counts. The [group-aware split script](../scripts/stage2e_split_approved.py) requires actual approvals, corrected six-class labels, rights evidence and resolved final group IDs before it can write manifests. Target **70% train / 20% independent test / 10% validation**, with every class represented in each, and prioritize leakage prevention. Freeze the independent test manifest and hash before training or tuning; use validation for model selection. Do not reuse the ZIPs' own splits blindly.

## Later scratch training and evaluation

When the dataset gate passes, construct the six-class YOLO11n architecture from `YOLO("yolo11n.yaml")` or the project's verified six-class YAML, set `pretrained=False`, and record `resume=False`, random seed, dataset/split version and hashes, architecture/config hashes, environment, RTX 3050 6 GB configuration and evidence that no `.pt` weights were loaded at initialization. Do not train on this candidate manifest.

On frozen test data, report the mentor's twelve parameter categories: TP, FP, FN, precision, recall, F1, AP@50 per class, AP@75 per class, mAP@50, mAP@50–95, mean matched-box IoU, and latency/FPS, with thresholds, support and formulas. Also produce raw/normalized confusion matrices, per-class results, PR and confidence curves, training/validation loss graphs, per-prediction confidence CSV, annotated examples and failure analysis. [The evaluation plan](../docs/evaluation_plan.md) specifies the protocol. Conventional accuracy/specificity requires a separately defined image-level presence/absence test with verified negatives. No metric, matrix or training artifact exists yet.
